# AI Industry Research & Provider Comparison

## Project Overview

This task explores current AI industry developments, production AI, cost optimization, token usage and major AI providers.

## Research Topics

1. Production AI and AI Agents
2. Cost Optimization and Token Usage
3. AI Provider Comparison

## Recent Article

A recent Reuters article about the rapid growth of enterprise AI voice agents was studied.

The research analyzes:

- Technology discussed
- Business impact
- Developer skills
- Production AI trends

## Mini Practical

OpenAI
Claude
Gemini
Groq

Comparison criteria:

Cost
Speed
Accuracy / Capability
