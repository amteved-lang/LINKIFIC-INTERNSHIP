HearMe is an AI-powered voice agent platform designed to automate customer communication.

HearMe uses Speech-to-Text technology to convert customer speech into written text.

Automatic Speech Recognition performance can be evaluated using Word Error Rate, Character Error Rate, and inference time.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation helps HearMe retrieve relevant company information before creating customer responses.

The platform can use Large Language Models to understand customer questions and generate natural-language responses.

Text-to-Speech converts generated responses into spoken audio for customers.

Routine customer-support queries can be handled automatically.

Complex or unresolved customer issues can be escalated to a human customer support agent.

HearMe can use company documentation and approved knowledge sources to provide grounded customer-support responses.
