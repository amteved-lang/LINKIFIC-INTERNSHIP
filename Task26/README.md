# Responsible AI & Advanced RAG Proof of Concept

## Project Overview

This project explores Responsible AI concepts including AI ethics, hallucination, bias, privacy, accountability, and human oversight.

The selected technical specialization is Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

## Specialization

Advanced RAG

## Proof of Concept

A responsible RAG workflow was implemented with:

- Approved company knowledge
- Information retrieval
- Source attribution
- Safe refusal
- Privacy redaction
- High-risk request detection
- Human escalation

## Workflow

User Question
↓
Privacy Check
↓
Risk Check
↓
Knowledge Retrieval
↓
Confidence / Relevance Check
↓
Grounded Answer or Safe Refusal
↓
Human Escalation when Required

## Responsible AI Case Study

The project studies Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149.

The incident demonstrates the risks of incorrect information being delivered through an automated customer-service system and reinforces the responsibility organizations have for AI-assisted outputs.

## Conclusion

Responsible AI requires technical safeguards and organizational governance.

Advanced RAG, safe refusal, privacy protection, source grounding, monitoring, and human oversight can reduce the risks associated with AI-powered customer-support systems.
