# HearMe Responsible AI Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines define responsible AI practices for AI systems used within the HearMe platform.

## 1. Ground Customer Answers

Customer-facing responses should use approved company information whenever factual company knowledge is required.

## 2. Do Not Invent Information

If reliable information cannot be retrieved, the AI should clearly state that it cannot provide a verified answer.

## 3. Human Escalation

High-impact or sensitive requests must be escalated when appropriate.

Examples include:

- Payments
- Refunds
- Legal questions
- Account changes
- Complaints
- Sensitive customer situations

## 4. Protect Customer Privacy

Collect and process only information required to perform the requested task.

Sensitive information should not be exposed in logs or unnecessary prompts.

## 5. Obtain Appropriate Authorization

Customer-specific databases and actions should only be accessed when the user and system are properly authorized.

## 6. Reduce Bias

AI performance should be evaluated across:

- Different languages
- Accents
- User groups
- Communication styles

## 7. Maintain Transparency

Users should know when they are interacting with an AI-powered system.

## 8. Provide Traceability

Important AI responses should be traceable to:

- Knowledge source
- Model/version
- Tool calls
- Relevant logs

## 9. Monitor AI Performance

Track:

- Incorrect responses
- Retrieval failures
- Escalations
- User complaints
- Latency
- Privacy incidents
- Unexpected model behavior

## 10. Test Before Deployment

AI features should be tested for:

- Accuracy
- Hallucination
- Bias
- Privacy
- Security
- Failure cases
- Prompt injection
- Unsupported questions

## 11. Maintain Human Accountability

AI systems assist business processes.

Final responsibility remains with the organization operating the system.

## 12. Continuous Improvement

Responsible AI evaluation should continue after deployment.

New failures and user feedback should be used to improve models, prompts, retrieval systems, tools, and policies.
